Delete Account – Fim It

Fim It respects your right to control your data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

What will be deleted

Upon request, we will permanently delete:

  • Analytics data associated with your app usage (such as hotspot clicks, link clicks, and interaction counts)
  • Any internal identifiers linked to your usage, if applicable

Fim It does not collect personal information such as names, phone numbers, passwords, or payment details.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Fim It account and associated data, please send an email to:

📧 fimdevelopers@gmail.com

Please use the subject line:
“Delete My Fim It Account”

If applicable, include any information that helps us identify your data (for example, the email address used within the app).

Processing time

Account and data deletion requests are processed within 7–14 business days. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel subscriptions. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Need help?

If you have questions about account or data deletion, contact us at fimdevelopers@gmail.com.
